Our services

Choose the groom your dog needs.

From a puppy's first introduction to a full transformation, services are tailored to your dog's coat, condition and grooming requirements.

01

The Puppy Prep

A gentle introduction to grooming, helping puppies become comfortable with the sights, sounds and sensations of the salon.

02

The Essentials

Bath, brush and dry for dogs who need a freshen-up without a full haircut.

03

The Trim & Tidy

Feet, feathering and sanitary areas tidied for dogs who don't need an all-over cut.

04

The Total Groom

A complete bath, brush, dry and all-over haircut, tailored to your dog's coat and preferred finish.
